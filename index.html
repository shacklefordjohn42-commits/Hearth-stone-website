I know what it means to work hard for what you have, and I don’t take it lightly when someone trusts us with their home or their hard-earned money.

My goal isn’t to earn one job. It’s to earn your trust for a lifetime.

That’s the kind of business I believe in, and that’s the kind of business we’re building.

Founding Project Manager / Builder

Joe Hamrick brings the experience of a seasoned builder, the eye of a craftsman, and the kind of work ethic that’s becoming harder to find.
